About Me

I was a bit aimless as a college student until I found my love for English. Then I found rhetoric and composition, and everything changed. Forever.

I teach writing & rhetoric and technical communication, and I’m a doctoral student at Texas Tech University, pursuing a Ph.D. in Technical Communication and Rhetoric. I hold an M.A. in Rhetoric and Composition from Texas State University.

My interests vary from Thai rhetoric and TV to queerness at large, and from creative pedagogies that bring students’ attention back into the process, to what AI does to language and meaning.

Classes I Teach

Writing & rhetoric

Exploring ideas through critical reading, purposeful writing, and revision, with attention to audience, context, and purpose.

Research & argument

Developing persuasive arguments through inquiry, careful evaluation of sources, and responsible use of evidence.

Technical communication

Creating clear, usable documents for professional audiences, with an emphasis on ethical communication and effective design.

Writing for international students

Helping international students navigate the expectations of college writing and develop their academic voices.

Teaching formats

In person, synchronous online, and asynchronous online.
