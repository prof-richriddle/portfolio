Who I am

I teach first-year writing and technical communication, and I’m a Ph.D. student in Technical Communication and Rhetoric at Texas Tech University. I came to academia after a career in operations and pricing management in international freight forwarding. My research looks at Thai rhetoric as a system, with current work on conduct rhetoric, AI literacy, and what commercial machine translation does to culturally specific meaning.

What I’m doing now

  • Finishing doctoral coursework and preparing for qualifying exams
  • Writing a book chapter on the Thai series Khemjira
  • Running two IRB-approved studies
  • Teaching writing courses online
  • Serving on committees that write AI and technical communication guidance for faculty

How I work

In my classes, anyone can point out a mistake, me included. I would rather try something new and fail than give students a “safe” path that still falls short. I set the room up as a brave space: a place to say what you think and to hear, respectfully, what you don’t agree with. In research, I try to read a culture on its own terms before I compare it to mine.
