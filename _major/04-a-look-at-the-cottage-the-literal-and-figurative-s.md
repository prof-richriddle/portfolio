How does a TV show travel, who carries it, and what does that do to the relationships along the way? This study follows how the HBO series <i>Heated Rivalry</i> moved through a network of colleagues, friends, and partners. Participants fall into six tracks, from people who found the show on their own to people who picked it up secondhand without watching it. Early findings: the network itself is the site of study, my relationships with participants are part of the method, and people used the shared show for emotional regulation, holding their network together under political pressure.
