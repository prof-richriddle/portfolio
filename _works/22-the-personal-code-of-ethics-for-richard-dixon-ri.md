My code rests on four values: clarity, integrity, humor, and compassion for anyone who has to read technical documentation. Using Black gospel music as a running comparison, I set out duties to the requester, to the unknown future reader, and to myself, including refusing work that harms the vulnerable and staying aware of how technical knowledge has been misused.
