This presentation shows how I audited my own research grant proposal. A pilot test of แม่ค้า (mâe káa) showed that a licensed subtitle and three LLMs all lose its gender recognition. Three audit methods (a “mad-lib” test, a funder FAQ check, and three hostile reviewer personas) exposed gaps in the budget and timeline, and an equity rewrite recast Thai scholars as credited expert consultants instead of hourly validators.
