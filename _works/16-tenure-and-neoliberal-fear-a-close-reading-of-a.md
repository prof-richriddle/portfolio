When a tenured professor’s speech goes viral, how fast and how uniformly a university responds can be read as a sign of neoliberal fear. Drawing on Gerdes’s <i>Sensitive Rhetorics</i> and Ahmed’s <i>Complaint!</i>, I build a matrix of five fears (collective solidarity, incivility, structural indictment, utopian demand, and public good versus commodities) and use it to close-read one such speech. Structural indictment appears most often.
