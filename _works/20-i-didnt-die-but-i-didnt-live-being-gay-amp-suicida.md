This autoethnography returns to my sixth-grade year to look at how five systems responded to a gay, suicidal child: school, an outpatient mental health facility, the church, child protective services, and my family. The systems worked at first, because I survived. Afterward they failed, labeling me learning disabled instead of making room for who I was.
