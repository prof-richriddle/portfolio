Using Blakeslee and Fleischer’s <i>Becoming a Writing Researcher</i>, I trace my path as a researcher: leaving a restrictive Oneness Apostolic church in 2019, finding Thai BL through <i>SOTUS</i> and <i>Love Sick</i>, and writing my MA autoethnography as a fan. I close by asking how much, if any, of that story belongs in a dissertation at all.
