Group project with four classmates. A class presentation on Chinelo Okparanta’s novel <i>Under the Udala Trees</i> (2015), covering the author’s background and awards, the Biafran War, and Nigeria’s laws against same-sex relationships, including the 2014 Same-Sex Prohibition Act, followed by discussion questions on religion, diaspora, narrative style, and the queer body.
