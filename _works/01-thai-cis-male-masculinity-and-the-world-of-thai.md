Thai BL began with <i>Love Sick: The Series</i> (2014) and grew from fan-subtitled uploads into a genre watched around the world. This paper traces three founding series (<i>Love Sick</i>, <i>SOTUS</i>, and <i>TharnType</i>) and the debates international fans have had over calling one man in a couple a “wife.” I argue that Western fans tend to police their own gaze while trying to read the genre on Thai terms, while Filipino fans, as Baudinette describes, “creatively misread” it into what they need. Since so little scholarship addresses Thai BL directly, I propose an interdisciplinary approach centered on rhetorical analysis.
