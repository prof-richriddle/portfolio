The usual arguments for English-only technical communication (safety, efficiency, education) leave out most of the world. This presentation centers on one scene from the Thai series <i>Secret Crush on You</i> (2022), where the subtitler adds a translator’s note: Touch calls Daisy แม่ค้า (mâe káa), the word for “vendor” used only for women, and in doing so affirms her identity. Google Translate won’t give you that, so who are we trusting to keep us connected to the world?
