Written for <i>Technical Communication Quarterly</i>, this review reads Baudinette’s argument that Thai BL has displaced Japanese BL as the leading form of Asian queer popular culture. I argue that the book, without using the field’s language, studies genre ecology and multiplatform information architecture, and that its “creative misreading” names something communication scholars have circled without coining.
