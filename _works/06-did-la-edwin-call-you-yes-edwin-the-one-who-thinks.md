Luis Negrón’s <i>Mundo Cruel</i> is a collection of darkly funny short stories about queer life in Puerto Rico. Using Berlant and Warner on heteronormativity and Asencio on Puerto Rican gay masculinities, I read “La Edwin,” “Botella,” “The Vampire of Moca,” and “The Chosen One” for how both straight and gay norms constrain queer Latin bodies. I argue that the collection refuses to cement its characters in definitions and instead shows every queer person as different and exactly the same: human.
