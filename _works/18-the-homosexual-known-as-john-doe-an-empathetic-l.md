Framed as a church service, this personal essay traces how a restrictive Oneness Apostolic upbringing built a “self-imposed imprisonment” around John Doe’s sexuality, and how empathy for fictional characters in Thai BL helped start his healing. The order of service runs through leaving organized religion while trying to keep faith, coming out with no more compartmentalizing, finding a new faith and a new “ideal” (Freud’s ego-ideal), and confronting negative self-talk.
