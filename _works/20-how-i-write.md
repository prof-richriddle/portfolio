I speak and write in different registers and accents depending on who is listening: church elders, my mother, my sister, academic friends, my Thai teacher. This short reflection traces those registers back to a multicultural church in Austin with members from over 30 countries, and to Texas public schools, and explains why I audited a grammar course while writing my thesis.
