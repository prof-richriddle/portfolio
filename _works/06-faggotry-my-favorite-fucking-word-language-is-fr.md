If knowledge is power and knowledge requires language, then gaining or destroying power means gaining or destroying language. Drawing on Boisen and Murray’s “apartheid of thought” and Baker-Bell’s <i>Linguistic Justice</i>, I argue that a white, patriarchal hegemony in the US works to deny, suppress, and obscure the language of marginalized groups. Alongside my own path to finding words for myself as a gay man, I look at the Deaf President Now movement and the classroom, and I close by reclaiming the word in the title.
