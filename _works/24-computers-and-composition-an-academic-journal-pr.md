A profile of <i>Computers and Composition</i> based on volumes 80 (a 40th-anniversary special volume) and 81, which hold 28 research articles. It maps the journal’s scope, from composing in AI-mediated environments to writing pedagogy, critical AI literacies, public rhetoric, and human-centered design, and shows how AI is reopening its long-running debates about access, agency, and power.
