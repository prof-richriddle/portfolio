Thai rhetoric, วาทวิทยา (wâat wít-tá-yaa), is its own system, not an accent of the Aristotelian tradition. This presentation starts from one word, เตือน (dteuuan), where an LLM subtitle turned a binding directive into a passive “reminded,” and argues that a well-formatted subtitle is not the same as a meaningful one. It introduces a 21-title comparative rhetoric reading list, drawing on Mao, Stroud, and Adsanatham.
