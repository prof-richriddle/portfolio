A complete course package for first-year college writing, with documents for students and documents for administrators and teachers. The course teaches expository writing through revision and critical reading of multimodal texts, including the films <i>The Fifth Element</i>, <i>Moonlight</i>, <i>A Raisin in the Sun</i>, <i>Inherit the Wind</i>, <i>Sicario</i>, and <i>Sonic the Hedgehog</i>. Its rationale gives everyone, teacher and students alike, permission to point out mistakes, and sets the classroom up as a “brave space.”
