  1. A class proposal

    Fall 2025: a semester project proposal on the Thai series Khemjira as a site for studying Thai rhetoric, written for a history of rhetoric seminar.

  2. A conference talk

    March 2026: “Khemjira Must Survive: Queer Longing and Thai Spirituality at the Intersection of Thai Rhetoric,” at the Gods and Monsters Conference.

  3. A book chapter

    Forthcoming: “Khemjira: The Queering of Thai Lakorn’s Cosmology,” in a collected edition from the conference (McFarland).
