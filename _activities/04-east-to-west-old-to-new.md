Students run a comparative analysis on two axes. **East to West:** Thai commercials beside U.S. commercials. **Old to New:** ads from the 1940s–1960s beside ads from today. For each pair, they look at how the ad persuades its audience, and what changes when the culture or the decade changes. It’s a lesson in transnational and comparative rhetoric.
