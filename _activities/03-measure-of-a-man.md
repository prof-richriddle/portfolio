Before watching an episode of *Star Trek: The Next Generation*, students read a short background page; no Star Trek knowledge needed. While they watch, they track how characters use technical language to describe a living being, which differences the characters think matter, and what follows when someone is labeled a “machine.” Then we hold a literal debate on the episode’s central question: what counts as a person?
