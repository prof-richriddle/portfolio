---
1. Students decide what makes something a Christmas movie, and explain why.
2. I build bingo cards from their list.
3. We watch *Die Hard*, and students mark their cards as the evidence shows up.
4. Students write a reflection on whether *Die Hard* is or isn’t a Christmas movie, using the rules they set at the start, without knowing that’s where it was heading.

It’s a lesson on genre: not just film genre, but rhetorical genre theory.
