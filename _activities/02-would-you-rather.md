A “Would You Rather” question forces a choice between two imperfect options, which is the core of argument: take a position, give a reason, defend it.

1. Students vote.
2. One student per side gives a claim and one reason.
3. I ask for the warrant (“What are you assuming?”) or the criterion (“What mattered most in your choice?”).
4. I ask who switched sides, and what persuaded them.

In writing and rhetoric, each answer maps onto Toulmin’s model: claim, reason, warrant. In technical communication, each question is a small decision problem, like a recommendation report: students name their criteria (time, money, comfort, risk) and decide which matters most.
